## Detailed description

```
UnityChat merges the live chat of Twitch, YouTube and Kick into a single panel next to the stream. One list of messages instead of three tabs — and you can reply to any of them without leaving the stream.

━━━ WHAT IT DOES ━━━

• Unified chat — messages from all three platforms in one column, each tagged with a coloured platform badge
• Send and reply — type straight from the panel; the message goes out through the account you are already signed in with
• Emotes from everywhere — 7TV, BetterTTV, FrankerFaceZ, native Twitch and Kick emotes, including zero-width stacking
• Badges — subscriber, moderator, VIP and the rest render exactly like in the native chat
• Autocomplete — Tab completes an emote or an @name, and it knows your StreamElements bot commands too
• Mention highlighting — when someone types your name, the message is marked so you cannot miss it
• Pinned messages — a banner above the chat shows what the streamer pinned
• Raids and first-time chatters — incoming raids and new chatters stand out from the rest
• Nicknames — give anyone a custom name and colour that follows them across all three platforms
• Chat history — close and reopen the panel without losing the conversation
• Three interface sizes and a dark theme that does not fight with the stream next to it

━━━ HOW IT WORKS ━━━

Open a stream on Twitch, YouTube or Kick and click the UnityChat icon. The panel detects the channel you are on and connects. There is nothing to configure.

Messages are sent through the login you already have in your browser — UnityChat never asks for a password and has no account of its own.

━━━ PRIVACY ━━━

Chat history stays locally in your browser and is never uploaded. The only things sent to the UnityChat server are the username you are signed in with, the channel you are watching, and any nicknames you choose to set. Details: https://jouki.cz/UnityChat/privacy

━━━ NOTES ━━━

• YouTube chat is read through the stream's public interface — the stream has to be live; replays and premieres will not load
• Message pinning works on Twitch
• UnityChat is an independent project and is not affiliated with Twitch, YouTube or Kick

Ideas and bug reports: https://jouki.cz
```
